How it works

Four simple steps

From sign-up to your first collaboration, it takes minutes.

1

Verify with your college email

Sign in with your .edu address so everyone on the platform is a real student from your campus.

2

Add what you offer and what you want

List the skills you can teach and the ones you want to learn. These two lists power your matches.

3

Get matched with peers

Your feed and suggestions show people whose skills fit your goals, and who want what you know.

4

Chat, collaborate and rate

Send a collaboration request, plan a study session in chat, and rate each other afterwards to build trust.
